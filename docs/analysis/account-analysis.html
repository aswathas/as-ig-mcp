06

Five things the data says

1. Problem-solving beats production value.

The DPF reel is a phone video about a real car problem. It got 3,698 likes and 188 comments — more than every philosophical reel combined.

2. Comments follow usefulness.

The top reel’s 188 comments are owners asking about their own cars. Recent reels average zero comments because there is nothing to ask.

3. The audience is car-shaped.

89.7% men, 25–34, Chennai. AI hackathon content reached 2,031 plays but only 32 likes — reach without resonance.

4. The gap cost more than any one post.

41 posts in March, 17 in April, zero from May to September. When posting resumed, the ceiling was 32 likes.

5. The second lane is human stories.

Only 3 posts, but 83 average likes and the 154-like kindness reel. It works when it is a real person, not a quote card.

07

The next 30 days

A plan that follows the evidence instead of fighting it.

  1. Three reels a week, no gaps.Two automotive, one human story. Consistency is the cheapest fix available.
  2. Lead with a car problem people search for.DPF, mileage, used-car checks, “is this noise normal”. Searchable beats clever.
  3. One question in every caption.“What’s your car’s mileage?” gives the algorithm a reason to rank comments.
  4. Keep the Tamil, drop the generic tags.#fyp #viral add nothing. Use model names, city, and the problem.
  5. One human story a week, filmed, not designed.The vendor reel proved it. Say the feeling in the first line.
  6. Add a link in bio.There is none today. Every viral reel currently has nowhere to send people.
08

Method and limits

All 68 posts were read through Instagram’s private API on 10 November 2026: publish date, likes, comments, and media type. Play counts were pulled for the 12 highest-performing reels. Audience figures come from the account’s own follower graph and are reported as Instagram gives them — percentages for age, city and country, counts for gender.

Not available: per-reel reach and impressions for most posts, profile visits, and story counts. Instagram returned those endpoints empty for this account, so none of the numbers here are estimated.
